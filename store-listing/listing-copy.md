## Full description (≤4,000 characters)

```
Aegis connects people who want to volunteer with the organizations and community initiatives that need them.

For volunteers:
- Discover opportunities filtered by cause, skill and area — your feed is built around what you actually care about and can offer, not a generic list.
- See why a listing matched you: Aegis shows which of your causes or skills lined it up with a listing.
- Sign up in one tap, for both one-off Scheduled events (with a date, time and limited spaces) and standing Ongoing needs.
- Track your volunteering hours automatically — once an organization marks your sign-up complete, it's logged on your Hours page.
- Message the organization directly about a listing you've signed up for.
- Works offline: your feed and commitments are cached on your phone, and sign-ups made without a connection sync automatically once you're back online.

For organizations:
- Post Scheduled or Ongoing opportunities, tagged with the causes and skills you're looking for.
- See everyone who's signed up, message them directly, and mark their hours complete once the work is done.
- Reach the right volunteers automatically — Aegis notifies people whose interests or skills match a new listing.

Other features:
- Secure sign-in with Google or email/password, with biometric (fingerprint/face) unlock for quick, secure access.
- Real-time push notifications for new matching listings, new sign-ups, and completed hours.
- Available in English, Afrikaans and isiZulu.
- Light and dark theme support.

Aegis is built to make it easier for communities to help each other — whether that's an afternoon reading to kids, planting trees, or serving meals at a soup kitchen.
```
