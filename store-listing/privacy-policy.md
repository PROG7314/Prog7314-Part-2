# Aegis Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [fill in date before publishing]_

Aegis ("the app") is a volunteer-matching app that connects volunteers with organizations posting volunteering
opportunities. This policy explains what information Aegis collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google or with email/password (via Firebase Authentication), we
receive your name, email address and (if you use Google sign-in) your profile picture.

**Profile information you provide.** Your display name, bio/description, profile picture, and — if you're a
volunteer — the causes and skills you select.

**Volunteering activity.** Listings you create (if you're an organization), listings you sign up for, your
sign-up status, hours logged against completed commitments, and messages you send to other users about a
specific listing.

**Device and notification data.** A Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token tied to your account, used only to
deliver push notifications about events relevant to you (a new matching listing, a new sign-up, your hours being
logged). You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings.

**We do not collect:** precise or coarse location data, contacts, or any data beyond what's listed above. The
app only requests the Internet, network-state and notification permissions.

## How we use this information

- To operate the app's core features: matching volunteers to opportunities, managing sign-ups, tracking hours,
  and enabling messaging between volunteers and organizations.
- To send push notifications for events relevant to your account.
- To display your language preference (English, Afrikaans or isiZulu) and theme preference.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Where your data is stored

Account and app data is stored in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud infrastructure).
Profile and listing photos are stored with Cloudinary. All data in transit is sent over HTTPS, and all API
requests are authenticated with a signed token.

## Data retention and deletion

You can edit or remove your profile information at any time from the app. To request deletion of your account
and associated data, contact [team/support email to be filled in].

## Children's privacy

Aegis is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under the age
applicable in your jurisdiction.
